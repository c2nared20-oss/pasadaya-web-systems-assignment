About the System

Why we built PasaDaya

A closer look at the transport gap in Bacuag and the mobile-first solution we designed to close it.

The Problem

Booking a tricycle in Bacuag still relies on flagging one down

Commuters in the Municipality of Bacuag have no reliable way to estimate fare, track an incoming ride, or contact a driver ahead of time. Waiting times are unpredictable, and drivers have no organized way to reach passengers efficiently across barangays.

Local transport oversight is also largely manual — driver verification, complaint handling, and fare monitoring happen without a centralized digital system.

Our Solution

One connected system for riders, drivers, and local admins

PasaDaya pairs a Passenger app and a Driver app with a live matching and tracking system, plus a web Admin Dashboard that lets municipal staff verify drivers, monitor rides, and manage fare rates.

The result is a faster, safer, and more transparent tricycle-booking experience — built specifically around how Bacuag commutes.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Mission

To give every commuter and tricycle driver in Bacuag a simple, dependable digital tool for booking, tracking, and completing rides — reducing waiting time and improving road-side safety.

Vision

A municipality where local public transport is organized, transparent, and easy to trust — powered by technology built for the community it serves.

Core Values

What guided every design decision

Safety First

Verified drivers, visible ride tracking, and in-app communication so no trip feels uncertain.

Simplicity

Clear screens, minimal steps, and Filipino-first labeling so every commuter can use it comfortably.

Real-Time

Live location, live status, live pricing — decisions made with up-to-date information, always.
