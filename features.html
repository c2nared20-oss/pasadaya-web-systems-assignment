Features

Everything a booking needs, built in

From the moment a passenger opens the app to the moment the ride is rated, PasaDaya was designed to remove guesswork on both ends of the trip.

One-Tap Booking

Set pickup and drop-off points on the map and PasaDaya matches the nearest available driver automatically.

Live GPS Tracking

Watch the assigned tricycle move on the map in real time, from acceptance to arrival.

In-App Chat & Calls

Riders and drivers message or call each other directly inside the app to confirm pickup details.

Upfront Fare Estimate

See an estimated fare before confirming a trip — no surprises when you reach your destination.

Verified Drivers

Every driver account is reviewed and approved by an admin before it can accept rides.

Ride History

Every completed trip is logged with fare, route, and driver details for easy reference.

Broadcast Announcements

Admins can push municipality-wide notices — fare changes, advisories — straight to every user.

Admin Dashboard

A web console for monitoring active rides, managing users, and reviewing driver documents.

Ratings & Feedback

Passengers rate every ride, helping maintain quality and accountability across drivers.
