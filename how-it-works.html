How It Works

From booking to drop-off in six steps

Whether you're a passenger or a driver, PasaDaya keeps every ride simple and predictable.

1

Create an Account

Sign up as a Passenger or apply as a Driver. Driver accounts go through admin verification before activation.

2

Set Pickup & Drop-off

Pin your pickup point and destination on the map. PasaDaya instantly shows an estimated fare.

3

Get Matched with a Driver

The nearest available, verified driver receives the request and accepts the trip.

4

Track & Communicate

Watch the driver's live location and chat or call to coordinate the exact pickup spot.

5

Arrive & Pay

Reach your destination and settle the fare directly with the driver, just like a regular tricycle ride.

6

Rate the Ride

Leave a rating and feedback — helping keep service quality high across all PasaDaya drivers.
